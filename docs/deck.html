The gapTwo axes of collateral02 / 08

TradFi manages collateral on two axes. Chains only carry one.

AxisWhat it coversOn-chain lending
Price Mark, haircut, volatility Oracle, LTV, liquidation
Eligibility Redeemability, transferability, issuer control, instrument pause Effectively absent

The ECB defines eligible collateral in Guideline (EU) 2015/510 Part Four and national central banks vet instruments before they are usable. ICMA triparty repo maintains eligibility sets by grade; ISDA publishes jurisdictional eligible-collateral tables. Eligibility is a first-class control, not a footnote to price.

The correction we made

Our first framing — "the eligibility axis does not exist on-chain" — was wrong. Aave can set LTV to zero. Morpho curators can set caps to zero. Hypernative detects and pauses in seconds.

The accurate problem is narrower:

When an asset-level event fires, the parties holding that asset are unrelated third-party protocols. Their only paths are subscribing to the same vendor or acting by hand. No path lets the destination contract verify the event itself — and when asset and credit market sit on different chains, the gap widens.

Sources are cited in full in CROSS_CHAIN_COLLATERAL_ELIGIBILITY_PROJECT_STATE.md §6, §12. The ECB/ICMA/ISDA framing is documented; we did not obtain a verbatim clause stating eligibility is lost for non-price reasons, and do not claim one.

Negative demoWhat the deployed ledger refuses07 / 08

Four forgeries, four different refusals, on-chain — and the ledger never moved.

C and D never reach our code. The BlockProver precompile refuses them inside the Creditcoin runtime — which is the point of building on Attestcoin rather than re-implementing verification.

The whole demo runs as eth_call against the live contracts, so it cannot write, and it reads the ledger back afterwards to show IMPAIRED · impairedSince 25745732 · events 1 is unchanged.

Two rules worth arguing about

  • Gate new risk, never trap users. repay and withdrawCollateral stay open forever. Only new exposure is refused. A control that blocks the exit is a hostage situation.
  • Earliest proven impairment wins. A later proof never pushes the cutoff forward. Credit extended after the real event does not become sound because the proof arrived late.
LimitsWhat we do not claim08 / 08

The prior art is strong. Here is exactly what is left.

Not claimed

  • Not real-time protection. Measured attestation lag is 8–9 minutes. This is delayed but verifiable gating of new credit. Hypernative binds detection to pre-approved on-chain actions and responds in seconds — on speed we lose.
  • NO_PROOF is not a clean bill of health. Absence is unprovable. It means only that nothing has been proven here yet.
  • Not control over existing markets. Morpho Blue's core has no pause primitive at all — zero matches for pause/freeze/emergency/blacklist in Morpho.sol. We apply eligibility logic to a venue on CC3; we do not reach into other chains.
  • Discovery is not trustless. Choosing which transaction to submit still needs off-chain log scanning. Verification is trustless; discovery is not.

In every one of these the trigger is a human or a market-risk metric, and the destination trusts a vendor. What is missing is narrow and specific: an issuer-side event, carried across a chain boundary, that the destination credit contract verifies for itself. That is the primitive this repo ships.

Nobody-solved-this is not a claim we make. Mock ERC-20s stand in for the lent asset; the source event, the proof, the verification and the refusal are all real. Full limitation list and prior-art assessment in the repository.
